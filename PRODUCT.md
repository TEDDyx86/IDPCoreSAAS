# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Undergraduate students in CS and Engineering at Brazilian universities. They are ambitious, often managing 5+ concurrent course modules, and already ahead of their peers in self-organization. They configure IDP Core once, then return to the dashboard to consume auto-generated summaries and complete comprehension quizzes. Primary use case is pre-exam preparation and catching up on materials without friction. They interact in Portuguese (Brazil) and expect a tool that respects their intelligence.

## Product Purpose

IDP Core is an autonomous academic learning acceleration system. It monitors Canvas, Moodle, and GitHub repositories 24/7 for new content, auto-generates study guides and summaries using Google Gemini AI, and delivers comprehension quizzes after each material drop. The student does not hunt for content; the system hunts for them. Success means a student who opens the dashboard 30 minutes before an exam and has everything they need, organized and distilled, already waiting.

## Brand Personality

Energetic, gamified, motivating. The product rewards the discipline of showing up. Studying with IDP Core should feel less like labor and more like leveling up. The student who uses it is ahead of their class, and the interface should reflect that edge.

## Anti-references

- **Generic SaaS (Notion clones):** Flat whites, pastel accent buttons, Inter on everything, metric cards with gradient text. Institutional blandness. IDP Core should feel nothing like a project management tool dressed up as an education product.
- **Crypto/NFT dark UIs:** Neon on black with no restraint, purple gradients saturating every surface, stat panels stacked with glowing numbers for their own sake. High energy does not mean visual noise. Restraint is the difference between premium and chaotic.

## Design Principles

1. **Signal, not noise.** The system runs autonomously in the background. The dashboard surfaces only what demands attention. Empty space is trust, not absence.
2. **Reward the discipline.** Quizzes, streaks, and progress should feel earned. The interface acknowledges effort without condescending to it. No cartoonish celebrations; understated visual confirmation.
3. **Academic gravity, modern execution.** The material is serious university content. The execution should feel like a premium tool, not an LMS. The two are not in conflict.
4. **Motion communicates, not decorates.** Transitions signal system state: processing, loading, new content arriving. Animation that doesn't carry information should be cut.
5. **Gamification with restraint.** Progress mechanics motivate without becoming the main event. The content is the product; gamification is the engine beneath it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Key considerations: sufficient contrast ratios in the dark theme (especially dim/ghost text against dark surfaces), keyboard-navigable quiz interactions, and reduced-motion support for users with vestibular sensitivities given the animated aurora backgrounds./
